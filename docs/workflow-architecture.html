Deterministic scoring

WATCH-v1 is a research-priority score—not a probability

Arithmetic is fixed; evidence classification remains an analyst judgment that must be documented.

S = clamp(25P + 25F + 20M + 20C + 10X − 10R, 0, 100)

Bands: <45 Exploratory · 45–69.5 Research review · ≥70 Priority research. No score can activate a position.

P · 25Primary evidence
F · 25Fundamental tests
M · 20Market confirmation
C · 20Catalyst proximity
X · 10Expression quality
R · −10Counterdrivers, cap 2
P/F

Fundamental layer

Primary-source support and two predeclared tests prevent narrative drift.

M/C

Timing layer

Market confirmation and catalysts distinguish a valid thesis from an actionable research window.

X/R

Implementation layer

Expression quality raises confidence; independent counterdrivers reduce it without double-counting price action.

Notification policy

Alert only when a decision may change

Ordinary price noise and repeated breaches remain silent.

ConditionEngine actionControl rationale
New material supporting or contradictory evidenceAlert onceMay change thesis strength, risks or invalidation.
WATCH-v1 change of at least 10 points with new evidenceAlert onceCaptures meaningful research-priority changes.
Ordinary proxy thresholdRequire two completed sessionsAvoids intraday and single-close false positives.
Persistent breach already reportedSuppressState prevents repetitive alerts until the breach clears.
Passage of time or missing retrievalDo not infer stabilityAbsence of evidence is not evidence of normalization.
Exceptionally severe intraday eventProvisional risk reviewEscalation is clearly labelled and not treated as close confirmation.

Input contract

Each non-inference event requires a valid direct URL, publication date, observed timestamp, theme, direction and evidence kind. Market records must identify completed sessions.

Output contract

Each theme returns score components, market confirmation, evidence, counterdrivers, catalysts, invalidation, implementation risks and portfolio decisions—plus explicit research-only status.

Persistent state

seen_event_ids deduplicates evidence; active_breaches deduplicates market alerts and clears when price returns inside the threshold.

Recommended next architecture decision: implement the portfolio layer before any brokerage integration. The system should understand existing exposure, scenario loss and instrument costs before it can responsibly propose an executable position.
